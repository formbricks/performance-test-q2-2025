## Limitations and open gaps

- The abort made this a valid failure run, not a complete two-minute distribution or capacity limit.
- The built-in resource sampler hit a macOS Bash 3 empty-array bug; an explicit-selector sampler began
  at 18:21:33 UTC, so pre-run and early-peak container metrics are unavailable.
- Node network, CNI, conntrack, database wait-event, and Redis server metrics were not captured.
- The bounded load, stress, spike, and soak tiers were not run. Load remains blocked on a clean baseline;
  higher-impact tiers still require separate explicit approval.
- No Artemis configuration, scaling, or workload placement was changed during this test.
